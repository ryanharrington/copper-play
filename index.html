Copper

You are not the hero. You are the townie who sells the hero fish.

You arrive with five copper and no bed. Work, fish, dig, fight. Get a room, then a trade, then a house. Every number you see can go up, and every comfort has a better one above it.

How to play

Copper

Move. Tap the ground to walk there. Hold and drag anywhere to steer. On a keyboard, arrow keys or WASD.

Do things. Tap a building to go in. Tap a person to talk. Tap a creature to fight it. Every line in a panel says what it does before you do it.

Time. A day is four slots: morning, afternoon, evening, night. Most things take one. Buying, selling and eating take none. Sleep ends the day.

Sleep. Where you sleep decides your morning. Free places leave you stiff, chilled or ragged. A room makes your first action count double. Better beds do more.

Numbers. Brawn, Wits, Charm go up by doing. Fishing and Mining go up while the spot is orange or yellow. Money goes copper, silver, gold.

The point. Come from nothing. Get a bed, then a trade, then a house. The aims line at the bottom of the town always says what is worth doing next.
